# Task 12 — GEO and AI-Search Audit

## Goal

Assess whether answer engines can extract, trust, and cite Three Thai for key PVA
material, sourcing, testing, and application questions.

## Mode

Read-only audit. Do not edit files, browse the web, install packages, or commit.

## Inspect

- `answers`, `knowledge`, `applications`, product content, company evidence, and schema.
- Answer-first structure, definitions, comparisons, tables, sourcing, authorship,
  evidence, dates, entity consistency, and internal citation paths.

## Deliverable

Return:

1. AI-user question set grouped by intent.
2. Citation-readiness scorecard for current answer and knowledge content.
3. Missing evidence and trust signals.
4. Recommended answer modules and reusable content patterns.
5. Prioritized implementation tasks and dependencies.

## Acceptance criteria

- Do not claim actual ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or AI Overview visibility.
- Distinguish extractability, authority, and factual support.
- Flag any claim that needs owner-supplied proof.
