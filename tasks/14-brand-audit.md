# Task 14 — Brand, UX, and Information Architecture Audit

## Goal

Make the site communicate one distinctive, credible Three Thai brand story across
its most important routes and viewport sizes.

## Mode

Read-only audit. Do not edit files, start a browser, generate images, or commit.

## Inspect

- Global theme, typography, layout primitives, home sections, navigation, product,
  application, about, manufacturing, quality, and localized messaging.
- Brand consistency, visual hierarchy, information scent, repetitive copy,
  unsupported claims, generic exporter language, and obvious AI-style phrasing.

## Deliverable

Return:

1. Brand positioning diagnosis and proposed message hierarchy.
2. UX and information-architecture findings by page type.
3. Visual-system consistency risks observable from source.
4. Copy patterns to retain, revise, or substantiate.
5. Prioritized implementation tasks and dependencies.

## Acceptance criteria

- Do not infer rendered visual defects that source inspection cannot prove.
- Preserve factual constraints and localization scope.
- Treat global navigation, footer, theme, and company content as shared-file requests.
